# Shot Log — Privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

Shot Log is a personal espresso-logging app. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## Data the app stores

Everything you enter (shots, bags of beans, notes and settings) is stored **only on your
device**. The app has no server, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and it
does not send your data to the developer or anyone else.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose **Connect Google Drive**, the app signs in with your Google account and asks for
the `drive.file` permission. This lets it create and update **only the files it creates itself**:
a backup file named "Shot Log backup.csv" in a "Shot Log" folder in your Drive. It cannot see or
change any other files in your Drive. The app uses your email address only to show which account
is connected.

The backup is stored in your own Google Drive under your control. You can delete it at any time,
and you can disconnect the app in Settings or revoke its access at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Exports

**Export CSV** creates a file and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share menu. Where
it goes from there is up to you.

## App updates

The app downloads code updates from Expo's update service (expo.dev). These requests contain
technical information needed to deliver the update (such as the app version and platform), not
your shot data.
